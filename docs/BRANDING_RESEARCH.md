# Bidently — Branding & Name Research

Summary of naming research conducted for the product. This document records the
candidate names considered, the rationale for the chosen name, and the
outstanding checks to complete before commercial commitment.

## Chosen name

**Bidently** — *"Forge winning bids, faster."*

Bidently was chosen because "Forge" signals craftsmanship and engineering (useful
for a portfolio narrative), and the name works in both "bid" (US/global RFP
terminology) and "tender" (Pakistan/UK/Commonwealth terminology) markets without
alienating either.

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Notes |
|---|---|
| TenderMind | Strong on the tender lexicon; weaker on the global RFP market |
| WinRoom | Vivid but ambiguous — does not communicate the compliance/productivity value |
| BidPilot | Clear but generic; already crowded in the bid-tooling space |

## Outstanding checks before commercial commitment

- **Domain availability** — check `bidently.com` / `bidently.io`.
- **Trademark search** — check the trademark register in target markets
  (US, EU, UK, Pakistan) for "Bidently" and close variants.
- **Collision review** — confirm no existing product uses the name in the
  proposal-automation or adjacent categories.

These checks are not included in the product blueprint and are a prerequisite
before any public launch or sale.
